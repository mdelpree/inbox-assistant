Inbox Assistant

A private assistant that runs on its owner's own hardware. It is not a public service and has a single user.

What it does

  • Gmail: moves likely junk out of the inbox into a label called "Probably junk". It never deletes email.
  • Google Calendar: reads events to help plan the day (when enabled).

How your data is handled

  • Processing happens on a local device with a local AI model. No email or calendar data is sent to third parties.
  • Only sender, subject, a short preview and a few headers are read, never full message bodies.
  • Only message ids and sorting decisions are stored.
