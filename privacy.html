Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Who uses this app

Inbox Assistant is a personal, self-hosted tool used only by its owner with their own Google account. It is not offered to the public. The owner talks to it through a private Telegram bot that answers only the owner's Telegram account.

Google data accessed

  • Gmail: sorting (scope gmail.modify): reads message metadata (sender, subject, list headers, Gmail category) and Gmail's short snippet; adds and removes labels. The app never deletes or trashes messages and never sends email.
  • Gmail: summaries (same scope): when the owner asks, and each morning at 08:30, the app reads the full text of the emails the sorter kept in the inbox as needing the owner, and summarizes them. The text is read on the owner's device only.
  • Google Calendar (scopes calendar.readonly and calendar.events): reads events on the owner's main calendar to show the schedule and find free time. It adds, moves or deletes an event on that calendar only after the owner approves the exact change in Telegram; without an approval nothing changes. It never adds guests and never sends invitations or updates to anyone. Events that have other guests are not changed.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is processed on the owner's own device by a locally running AI model. No cloud AI service is used.
  • Data leaves the device only to Google (the owner's own account) and to Telegram (the owner's conversations with the bot). It is not sent to any other service.
  • The mail sorter stores only Gmail message ids and sorting decisions in a local database. It stores no email text.
  • Email text read for summaries is never logged or saved by the tools that read it; they keep only the time of the last summary. The text does appear in the assistant's conversation history on the device, which is deleted after 30 days (see below). Only the summary reaches the owner, through Telegram.
  • Tasks and reminders the owner creates are stored on the device, together with a log of the assistant's tool requests (for example task and event titles, never email text).
  • OAuth tokens are stored on the device with owner-only file permissions.
  • Google user data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising or to train AI models.

Telegram

Messages between the owner and the bot, including summaries, calendar details and approval requests, pass through Telegram's servers and are kept by Telegram under its own privacy policy. Telegram bot chats are not end-to-end encrypted.

How long data is kept

  • The assistant's conversation history on the device (chats, summaries and the messages sent by scheduled jobs) is deleted automatically after 30 days. A daily job removes anything older.
  • Memory notes the assistant saves about the owner (such as preferences) are kept until the owner asks for them to be removed.
  • Tasks, reminders and the tool-request log are kept until the owner removes them.
  • Messages in the Telegram chat itself stay in Telegram until the owner deletes them there.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local data folder removes everything it stored on the device. Messages in Telegram are deleted in the Telegram app.
